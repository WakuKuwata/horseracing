## Interpretation

No design blocker. The model still contains market information through q, other market columns and original price-based training weights. Call this a three-column ablation, never removal of market dependence. Dropping correlated market columns can alter tree splits and feature subsampling but adds no information and may weaken useful price signal. Distinguish lower NLL, improved same-parent ROI and surpassing original③ full normalization on **both** ROI/profit. Keep `can_adopt=false`, `eligible_for_verdict=false`, `untouched_holdout=false`; no production/UI/DB updates. The old time-specific odds acquisition remains held.
